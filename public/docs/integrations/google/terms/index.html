Terms of Use

These Terms of Use describe the limited use of Aito Nurmi Integrations for its current Google Search Console and Tag Manager connections and the preparation of a planned owner-only local Drive read pilot.

Users and purpose

The integration is a tool for the owner and an authorized operator. It does not provide public registration or a paid subscription. A user may authorize only Google resources for which they have the necessary authority.

Permissions and ownership

This description covers Search Console and Tag Manager read-only permissions. It does not permit editing or publishing in Tag Manager or transfer ownership of a Google account, site or container. The application name does not authorize additional providers, APIs or permissions.

Planned local Drive pilot boundary

The owner has approved preparation of a local read pilot, but the pilot is not activated. The proposed permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. It would cover all files accessible to the account, not just a selected folder, and does not grant write, delete or sharing rights.

Before activation, secure credential handling and the local data path must be verified. Account selection, Google's consent and the owner's choice of one harmless test file remain required. The pilot does not authorize Drive file data to be supplied to a cloud model, other assistant profiles, uploads or changes to existing Google integrations. Operational test status may enter assistant history and the configured cloud model service as described in the Privacy Policy.

Publishing these pages does not create a Google client, grant a permission or establish a working Drive connection. The pilot creates no uninterrupted-service or automatic-deletion guarantee.

Connection availability and results

Google authorization can be revoked. Revoked or otherwise invalid authorization may require fresh consent. An uninterrupted connection, a particular retention period or automatic deletion is not guaranteed.

Reports depend on information returned by Google. Delays, missing data and API limits may restrict results. A report must not be treated as complete when its coverage is incomplete.

Costs and external changes

The owner's operating rule requires a cost-incurring action, its pricing basis and any recurring or usage-based charges to be presented and separately approved before execution. Approval of a technical change is not approval of spending.

These documentation pages do not change Google or Cloudflare billing, permissions, domains or the operation of the local integration.

Privacy and limitations

Data handling and unverified practices are described in the Privacy Policy. Publishing documents does not demonstrate Google OAuth Production status, Google approval, Limited Use compliance or verified provider training practices.
