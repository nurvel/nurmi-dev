Terms of Use

These Terms of Use describe the limited use of Aito Nurmi Integrations for its Google Search Console and Tag Manager connections, the earlier local-only Drive read pilot, a separately enabled optional owner-only Drive tool and planned personal Gmail, Calendar and Tasks connections.

Users and purpose

The integration is a tool for the owner and an authorized operator. It does not provide public registration or a paid subscription. A user may authorize only Google resources for which they have the necessary authority.

Permissions and ownership

Search Console and Tag Manager use read-only permissions. They do not permit editing or publishing in Tag Manager or transfer ownership of a Google account, site or container. The separate optional Drive and planned personal-service permissions are described below; the application name does not authorize additional providers, APIs or permissions.

Earlier local-only Drive read pilot

The earlier local-only read pilot used https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly for one owner-selected operational test. That permission does not grant write, delete or sharing rights. Its pass/fail-only local test did not authorize broader content processing or writing.

Optional owner-only Drive tool boundary

When enabled after separate Google consent and operational checks, the tool requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive for all files accessible to the account, not a folder-only permission. Use remains limited to individually requested metadata discovery, selected downloads, folder/file creation, supported editing, single-file rename/move and named checkpoint copies. A checkpoint is a separate copy, not a named native revision or a guarantee that Google retains every edit.

The broad Google scope can technically permit deletion and sharing. The tool does not support deletion, trashing, sharing, ownership changes, bulk changes, background collection or other assistant profiles. A move can change inherited access through the destination folder and needs explicit acknowledgment. Initial editing is limited to UTF-8 text files and Google Docs text with a 1 MiB input limit; unsupported formats and folder/shared-drive moves must be rejected clearly.

Only information needed for an individually requested task may be supplied to the owner's Hermes conversation and its OpenAI model service, subject to the Privacy Policy and a check of provider data-use suitability before transfer. A request to manipulate a file is not blanket permission to scan Drive, train a model, publish its content or send it to other recipients.

Publication does not activate the tool, grant permissions or establish that an account is connected. The operator must verify secure credential handling, the permitted data path, exact scope/account, persistence and refresh before use. The owner personally completes Google's consent. These pages do not promise uninterrupted service, permanent version history or automatic deletion.

Planned personal Gmail, Calendar and Tasks boundary

When separately enabled, Gmail permits only requested search and message reading; it does not authorize sending or modifying email. Calendar and Tasks are for reading, creating and editing data for an individually requested task. Their broad write permissions do not authorize deletion, clearing lists, bulk changes, calendar sharing or access-control administration. Keep is excluded.

Each actual change requires a specific owner request and target. Invitations, participant notifications, recurring-event edits and effects on other people must be resolved before executing a change; an authentication grant alone is not permission to perform those actions. An uncertain write must be reconciled against its exact target before any retry.

Only necessary task-specific data may be supplied to the owner's Hermes conversation and its OpenAI model service under the Privacy Policy and after checking provider data-use suitability. This is not approval for automatic mailbox, calendar or task scanning, public disclosure or other external recipients.

Publication does not activate these connections, verify the owner's account, or prove that creating and editing tools are implemented or live-tested. Exact grants, secure storage, persistent reload, refresh and the intended consumer must pass operational checks. The owner personally completes Google's account selection and consent. Existing Drive grants must remain separate; separate clients within the same Google application do not isolate app-wide revocation or provider policy effects.

Connection availability and results

Google authorization can be revoked. Revoked or otherwise invalid authorization may require fresh consent. An uninterrupted connection, a particular retention period or automatic deletion is not guaranteed.

Reports depend on information returned by Google. Delays, missing data and API limits may restrict results. A report must not be treated as complete when its coverage is incomplete.

The owner's operating rule requires a cost-incurring action, its pricing basis and any recurring or usage-based charges to be presented and separately approved before execution. Approval of a technical change is not approval of spending.

These documentation pages do not change Google or Cloudflare billing, permissions, domains or the operation of the local integration.

Privacy and limitations
